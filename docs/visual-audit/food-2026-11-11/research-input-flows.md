# How food-tracking apps let a person log food: Mobbin survey

Date: 2026-11-11. Source: Mobbin (iOS), through the Mobbin search tools. Every statement below comes from a screenshot opened and read in this session. Where a thing was not in a screenshot, the row says "not seen". Screenshots cannot show motion, so nothing here says whether an app animates an add.

Links: `https://mobbin.com/screens/<id>` is one screenshot, `https://mobbin.com/flows/<id>` is the flow it belongs to.

## 1. Coverage

**Ten apps with a real logging flow seen** (these are the "of 10" in every tally): MyFitnessPal, Cal AI, Yazio, Lifesum, MacroFactor, BitePal, Noom, Google Health (Fitbit), Ultrahuman, Bevel.

**Two apps seen in part** (not counted in tallies): Life Reset (2 screens), WHOOP (1 screen).

**Searched by name and not returned by Mobbin** (the search gave other apps' screens): WeightWatchers (WW), Fastic, Foodvisor, FatSecret, Carb Manager, MyNetDiary, Fitia, SnapCalorie, Fooducate, Simple, Lose It!, Cronometer. **Zero** is on Mobbin, but a search for its meal logging returned only a profile screen (https://mobbin.com/screens/fc03aed2-1a4c-41af-9170-5f88a157ad1e) and a privacy page, so no logging flow was seen.

Builds: MyFitnessPal appears in two builds. The current one has a centre plus and "Log" per meal; an older one has a blue tab bar and "ADD FOOD" per meal. Rows say which. The MyFitnessPal account captured was Premium ([Plans screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/74f578aa-27fa-430d-90f7-6dfa64046f4b)), so a free account's locks could not be seen.

## 4. Where shipping apps and the planned design differ

Planned design, as given: four meal segments above one free-text field; a camera icon in the field for a paid AI scan; a pencil for manual entry; usual foods as suggestions while typing; a save adds a row to one time-ordered diary and a picture flies to the calorie ring.

Most common first.

1. **Foods to tap before any typing: 7 of 10, with a one-tap plus on each row in 8 of 10.** The plan shows the person's usual foods only once they type. MFP https://mobbin.com/screens/78c2c303-3a11-4cba-b8fb-c8be1fd68f3f, Yazio https://mobbin.com/screens/2d0882a2-7d29-4380-ac7f-62fd08bfa2e2, MF by hour https://mobbin.com/screens/91d37b5b-39a5-4add-a11a-e31dc1fb8a3f, Cal AI https://mobbin.com/screens/e363d6fb-4e38-49e3-996d-b3b6a9c5b67f, BitePal https://mobbin.com/screens/d13b87f1-bb1f-4daa-8182-58f837bf3967, Lifesum by meal https://mobbin.com/screens/0d1bb0df-9447-4e70-ab80-d69ebdde6365, Noom https://mobbin.com/screens/6c9ad2b4-29ff-4cf2-86fd-d540578ae7c0.
2. **Several foods in one visit: 8 of 10.** The person stays on the add surface and sees a tick, a count or a plate. Four save on each tap; four collect a plate and save once. The plan describes one save per entry. Yazio https://mobbin.com/screens/b0efe6c3-a8fc-492c-9553-a5cb506fc3c2, MF https://mobbin.com/screens/17107624-1399-4332-ba88-4dd81a1df947, MFP https://mobbin.com/screens/bbdbd9fe-7552-4a38-8601-da67f1741ea9, Noom https://mobbin.com/screens/9fa29164-9358-45fd-95a3-c6a52f95c708.

Closest to the plan's "pre-chosen by time, changeable" is MF's hour chip https://mobbin.com/screens/91d37b5b-39a5-4add-a11a-e31dc1fb8a3f and BitePal's "Meal" chip on its confirm sheet https://mobbin.com/screens/b4972476-48ad-491e-b136-83bd6eeb57b7.
