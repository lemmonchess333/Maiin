# Calorie wheels on Mobbin: what shipping apps do

Survey date: 11 November 2026. Source: Mobbin, iOS screens, 39 searches. 114
screenshots were downloaded at full size and read. Every statement comes from
a screenshot seen in that session; nothing is filled in from memory of an app.
Links have the form `https://mobbin.com/screens/<id>`.

Direction was decided only where the numbers on a screen allow it: the angle
of the coloured arc was measured in pixels and set against eaten over goal.
Diameter is the ring's outer diameter as a share of the screen width. Stroke
is its width as a share of the diameter: thin under 6%, medium 6 to 10%,
thick over 10%. "Not shown" means no screenshot on Mobbin shows that state.

## Coverage

- **Food apps with a calorie day view (12):** Cal AI, MacroFactor, Yazio,
  Lifesum, BitePal, MyFitnessPal, Noom, Life Reset, Alma, Bevel, Garmin
  Connect, Fitbit.
- **Not on Mobbin (searched by name):** WeightWatchers, Fastic, Foodvisor,
  FatSecret, Carb Manager, MyNetDiary, Fitia, SnapCalorie, Fooducate, Simple,
  Lose It!, Cronometer, Samsung Health, Athlytic. Zero has only a fasting
  timer.
- **Rings outside food (12):** Apple Fitness, Oura, WHOOP, Garmin Connect,
  Fitbit, Google Health (Fitbit), Withings, Gentler Streak, Bevel, Streaks,
  Tonal, Sweatcoin.

## Tallies

**Food apps (12)**

- Shape: full ring 3 (Cal AI, BitePal, Life Reset); arc 3 (MacroFactor, Yazio,
  Lifesum); bar 3 (MyFitnessPal, Noom, Garmin Connect); neither 3 (Alma,
  Bevel, Fitbit).
- Direction: fills with what was eaten 9; follows a switch 1 (MacroFactor);
  cannot tell 2 (Bevel, Fitbit); only empties 0.
- Number: switchable 3 (Cal AI, MacroFactor, BitePal); remaining 3 (Yazio,
  Lifesum, Bevel); eaten 6. Eaten and left both readable with no tap 7.
- The goal printed beside the number, among the 6 with a ring or arc: 5 (all
  but Yazio).
- Over target shown: 1 (BitePal).
- Empty day: a neutral track 6; a start dot 1 (Yazio); a full ring at zero 0.
- On the page 6; in a card 6. Of the six rings and arcs: 3 and 3.
- Widest ring: 47% of the screen (Lifesum). None is wider than half.
- Macros: bars 8; small rings 1 (Cal AI); other 3. A hue each 10; one hue 2.
- Exercise shown beside the gauge: 7.

**Rings outside food (12)**

- Fills with what is done 8; cannot tell 3; no gauge 1; empties 0.
- Remaining as the main number: 0.
- Over goal: a second lap 1 (Google Health); words on a full ring 3; a
  warning colour 0; not shown 8.
- On the page 8; in a card 4.

## Could not tell

- Direction for Bevel's calorie arc, Fitbit Food's needle, Zero, Streaks,
  Tonal and Sweatcoin.
- The tap itself in Cal AI and BitePal: only the chevron and both label
  states were seen.
- Calories over target in 11 of the 12 food apps, and in Apple Fitness.
- Alma's calorie disc level (near-white on white).
