# Hierarchy and type: rules measured from the apps people come back to

Area: how big the hero is, which text is the biggest thing on a screen, the display register, the type scale and page titles.
Folder: (working files, not committed)

## What was measured, and how

- **Sample.** 80 reference screens from 18 fitness and food apps, plus 2 consumer apps as a cross-check (Revolut and Headspace, labelled `xcheck`). That is 58 screens reused from `grill2/refs/` and 22 new ones from Mobbin, saved in `img/`. Each app is sampled on its main screens (home or today, a list, a detail, a reward, a live screen), not only its best ones. Breakdown: Nike Run Club 8, Hevy 7, Runna 7, Strava 7, Oura 6, Duolingo 6, Apple Fitness 5, WHOOP 5, Ladder 4, MacroFactor 4, Cal AI 3, Fitbod 3, Gentler Streak 3, Lifesum 3, Yazio 3, Bevel 2, Garmin 1, MyFitnessPal 1. **Tropos:** 18 dark captures from `grill2/current/*-fold.png` (393 × 852 pt at 2×), plus the 31 October finish screen.
- **Text.** `work/textlines.py` and `work/measure.py` find every text line: ink against the local background, components grouped into lines. For each line they record the **ink height** (baseline to the top of the tallest glyphs, in pt; it is the cap height for figures and capitals, the ascender height for mixed case) and the stroke width ÷ ink height (a weight proxy). A second pass on 64 pt background blocks finds the giant numerals. I checked every overlay by eye (`ov/ov-*.png`, sheets `crops/S00–S26.png` and `crops/T00–T05.png`). On each screen I picked the largest _text_ by hand from the five tallest candidates, dropping icons, rings, avatars, map labels and photo noise. Nine giant glyphs were measured on gridded crops (`crops/G1–G3.png`), and units on `crops/U1–U2.png`.
- **Geometry.** I read hero boxes off the 50 pt grids to about ±5 pt and give them as a share of the 852 pt first viewport, status bar included. The **object** is the dial, card, photo or figure. The **zone** is the object plus the headline, sentence and colour field that belong to it.
- **Conversions.** Font size ≈ ink height ÷ 0.75. This is calibrated on Tropos, whose sizes are known: 31 px "Today" measures 24.4 pt, 25 px "Pull A" 19.0 pt and 16 px body 11.5 pt. "Body" is the main reading text on each screen (sentences or list-row text, picked by hand). Its median is 11.2 pt in the references and 11.0 pt in Tropos, so ratios compare like with like.
- **Limits.** Sizes are clustered at 15% tolerance, so a count can be off by one. Weight is only reliable in three coarse classes, so no rule rests on it. The per-screen numbers are in the appendix tables, and `work/results.json` holds every value.

**Tropos today, in one line:** the hero is the right _size_ but the wrong _rank_. On Home the session card takes 22% of the viewport, inside the reference range. But the 31 px "Today" (24.4 pt ink) is bigger than everything in that card: "Pull A" is 19.0 pt and the 410 figure 17.5 pt. On 11 of the 18 captures the page title is the largest text. In the references that happens on 11 of 79 screens.

## 1. Rules

### Rule 1. Every main screen and every finish or reward screen has one hero, 30–60% of the first viewport

- **Rule:** Home, Train, Food, Analytics, Social, a profile and every finish or reward screen show one hero zone: one object plus its own words, 256–510 pt tall (30–60% of 852 pt), with its top within the first 260 pt. Screens that are lists of equal rows (the logger, settings, search, session history) may have none.
- **Evidence:** 63 of 80 reference screens have a single hero (79%).

  | Kind                       | Screens with a hero | Zone height, % of 852 pt (median, IQR, range) |
  | -------------------------- | ------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
  | Home / today (incl. day 1) | 24 / 30             | 34 (19–44), 13–64                             |
  | Detail                     | 16 / 18             | 30 (20–44), 11–84                             |
  | Reward                     | 14 / 15             | 45 (35–52), 22–62                             |
  | Live                       | 4 / 5               | 16 (9–75), 7–94                               |
  | List                       | 5 / 12              | 31 (23–36), 23–36                             |

  The object alone is a median 24% on Home (IQR 18–34). The zone's top on Home is a median 212 pt (IQR 114–238). Screens with no hero are the utility lists: Hevy 5 of 7, Fitbod 2 of 3, MacroFactor's food log, Yazio's diary, Strava's trophy case, and Garmin's home, the app in this set people tolerate rather than love. The largest zones are in the loved apps: Ladder's photo card is 55% ([ladder-home-today-photo-hero](https://mobbin.com/screens/0e0eb164-8560-4d10-b8bf-93017db938fa)), Oura's score with its headline 43% ([oura-today-readiness-80](https://mobbin.com/screens/bfe532d9-62fe-4246-9775-9c8934195bcb)), and Duolingo's node with its popover 44% ([duolingo-path-start-popover](https://mobbin.com/screens/3ecf07e8-cea8-4414-b5d9-05cd21033429)). The smallest is MyFitnessPal's 13% calorie card ([mfp-today-bars-diary](https://mobbin.com/screens/32b82d20-deb0-493f-a643-c170d9e46b8b)). WHOOP is the honest exception: a 115 pt dial row (18%) that reads as the hero through traffic-light colour, not area ([whoop-home-today](https://mobbin.com/screens/4e7d8b71-ac46-472f-8e31-b619f5e533ae)).

- **Why it works:** one object a size above everything else tells the person where to look first and what today is about, before they read. An even stack of cards makes them read every card to find that out.
- **Tropos today:** 10 of 18 captures have a hero, median 23% of the viewport. Home's session card is 188 pt (22%), at the low end of the reference range. On the established-account Train capture, Up next starts at y 555, so half of it is below the fold. Social Feed, Together, the profile, the league, the badges page, run setup and empty Analytics have none. The guide only defines a "Hero card" recipe (§6), with no size and no rule about where one is required.
- **Proposed wording:** "**One hero per main screen.** Home, Train, Food, Analytics, Social, a profile and every finish or reward screen open on one hero: the thing about you right now, with its own headline and sentence. Together they are 256–510 pt tall (30–60% of an 852 pt screen), and the top sits within the first 260 pt. A list of equal rows (the logger, settings, search, history) needs none. Everything after the hero is ordinary cards."

### Rule 2. The largest text on the screen is inside the hero, at least 1.2× the next size

- **Rule:** On any screen with a hero, the hero's biggest text (its figure or headline) is the largest text on the screen and at least 1.2× the ink height of the next-largest text, page title included.
- **Evidence:** on 50 of the 62 reference screens with a hero (81%), the largest text is in it: a hero figure on 29 screens, a hero headline on 22 (counts over all 80). A page title is the largest text on 11 of 79 screens (14%). Six of those have no hero (Apple's awards grid, Strava's trophy case, Hevy's empty home, Fitbod's day, MacroFactor's log, Headspace). The other five are MacroFactor's display title twice, NRC's tab titles, where "Run" (18.2 pt) ties its START (18.7 pt), and MyFitnessPal's "Today" (20.3 pt) over a 15.0 pt calorie figure. From the largest text to the next size, the median step is 1.39× (IQR 1.23–1.98). Examples: Oura's 80 is 37.7 pt under a small wordmark row ([oura-today-readiness-80](https://mobbin.com/screens/bfe532d9-62fe-4246-9775-9c8934195bcb)). MacroFactor's 1502 is 26.0 pt under its 21.3 pt title ([macrofactor-dial-remaining](https://mobbin.com/screens/05bca7bd-ff17-45dc-bca0-3b4c6d51a745)). Gentler's recap "24" is 110 pt under a 24.2 pt large title ([gentler-insights-recap](https://mobbin.com/screens/f1f313b1-01a0-4d8e-828b-dfc014130b13)).
- **Why it works:** the eye lands on the largest type first. If that is "Today" or "Train", the first thing read is a label the person already knew. If it is "Pull A" or "410", the first thing read is the news.
- **Tropos today:** on 5 of 10 hero screens the largest text is in the hero (Food 25.5 pt, Analytics 34.5, run detail 33.9, weekly review 34.5). On Home, Train and the plan, the title outranks the hero: 24.4 vs 19.0 pt, 22.6 vs 19–20, 22.2 vs 20.0. The guide sets both page titles and hero numbers at 800 (§4 weight rules) and gives the page title 31 px against the hero title's 25 px (§4 role table), so the guide itself produces this ranking.
- **Proposed wording:** "**The hero holds the largest type.** On a screen with a hero, its figure or headline is the biggest text on the screen, and at least 1.2× the next size. A page title never outranks it."

### Rule 3. A screen with a hero has no large title: the hero names the screen, or the title is a small row

- **Rule:** When a screen has a hero, it has no 31 px large title. The screen is named by the hero's own headline (the session, the day, the result), by a small title row (17 px semibold, ink height of 13 pt or less), or only by the date as a kicker. Large titles stay on index screens without a hero.
- **Evidence:** title treatment on 80 reference screens: none 26 (33%), small centred nav title 18 (23%, ink 8.7–17.7 pt, median 12.9), the hero carries the name 14 (18%), wordmark 12 (15%), large title 10 (13%). On the 30 Home screens: none 10, wordmark 9, large 5, nav 4, hero 2. The large titles measure 16.6–24.6 pt ink (median 20.8, about 28 pt type). They sit on index and tab-root pages: Apple's awards grid "Go For It" ([applefitness-awards-goforit](https://mobbin.com/screens/385c1711-6119-4b91-8ec2-50851648825a)), NRC's Run and Club tabs, Gentler's "Insights", MacroFactor's three tabs. Where a large title and a hero share a screen (8 screens), the title is as large as or larger than the hero's text on 6 (MacroFactor, NRC, MyFitnessPal), the ranking Rule 2 rejects. The apps whose heroes lead (Oura, WHOOP, Runna, Ladder, Duolingo, Bevel, Lifesum, Cal AI) use no large title on any sampled screen. The hero names the screen on Runna's workout ([runna-workout-detail-gradient-blocks](https://mobbin.com/screens/dd967604-1d7e-48b3-8596-8eb266005ba9)), NRC's poster ([nrc-guided-run-poster](https://mobbin.com/screens/fd3def39-c55c-4c88-a73b-4e0d06226aac)) and Gentler's rest day ([gentler-streak-home-rest-day-character](https://mobbin.com/screens/c991401a-cc03-4eb2-9143-e775e8b15865)). Only the two tolerated apps put a large title over a stack of cards on Home: Garmin's "Good afternoon, Sam" ([garmin-home-greeting-todays-activity](https://mobbin.com/screens/66366428-5782-43e0-92b6-b04b4fcd313a)) and MyFitnessPal's "Today".
- **Why it works:** a large title spends about 50 pt and the top rank of the type scale on a word the tab bar already says. Giving that rank to the hero makes the screen's first line the content, and moves the hero up.
- **Tropos today:** large title on 14 of 18 captures, at 22.2–24.4 pt ink (31 px, 800). §4's role table makes it universal ("Page title: text-h1 31px 800 — PageShell, SettingsSection"), and §1 says Home "is titled with the date and 'Today', like every other page names itself".
- **Proposed wording:** "**Titles give way to heroes.** A page with a hero has no large title. Either the hero's headline names the page (the session, the day, the result), or the page has a small title row (17 px, 600), or Home carries only the date as a kicker above the hero. The 31 px large title is for index pages without a hero: Settings, Badges, a list of sessions."

### Rule 4. Hero register: a figure at 40–48 px, a headline at 25–38 px, 1.6–3.2× body

- **Rule:** On Home and detail screens, a hero figure is set at 40–48 px (ink 30–36 pt, 2.5–3.2× body) and a hero headline at 25–38 px (ink 18–28 pt, 1.6–2.5× body). Nothing on these screens goes above 48 px.

  | Hero kind (refs)          | n   | Ink height, pt (median, IQR, range) | × body (median, IQR) | ≈ type size   |
  | ------------------------- | --- | ----------------------------------- | -------------------- | ------------- |
  | Home, figure              | 12  | 28.8 (19.5–32.9), 16.3–37.7         | 2.7 (1.9–3.1)        | 38 pt (26–50) |
  | Home, headline            | 7   | 17.7 (16.3–28.7), 13.3–32.9         | 1.8 (1.4–2.4)        | 24 pt (22–38) |
  | Detail and list, figure   | 8   | 27.1 (23.6–58.7)                    | 3.2 (2.4–5.3)        | 36 pt         |
  | Detail and list, headline | 7   | 18.0 (15.9–23.7)                    | 1.8 (1.4–2.0)        | 24 pt         |

- **Evidence:** no Home hero in the sample is above 37.7 pt ink (Oura's score, about 50 pt type). Figures: Cal AI's 2471 is 28.5 pt ([calai-home-dark-week-rings](https://mobbin.com/screens/0b587652-d78b-4abb-8d03-aa9685167524)), Lifesum's 220 is 29.3 pt ([lifesum-diary-filled-gradient-dial](https://mobbin.com/screens/ba02a832-1a7e-4717-8dae-e96cb104ad78)), and Bevel's sleep 67% is 26.2 pt ([bevel-sleep-detail](https://mobbin.com/screens/ec59eceb-c06f-4ac6-b132-1938e39bac5c)). Headlines: Runna's "Recovery done, Alex" is 17.7 pt ([runna-today-rest-day-recovery-done](https://mobbin.com/screens/48d9e879-8ad7-4c03-8483-8b064eb3a4f1)) and Ladder's "ARMS & DEEP CORE" 28.7 pt ([ladder-home-today-photo-hero](https://mobbin.com/screens/0e0eb164-8560-4d10-b8bf-93017db938fa)).
- **Why it works:** big enough to read at arm's length in a second, small enough to leave the hero room for its sentence and its button. On a screen people open daily, the size stays steady and the content changes.
- **Tropos today:** already inside this register. "Pull A" is 19.0 pt (25 px), the Food ring's 410 is 25.5 pt, Analytics' 92 is 34.5 pt (48 px), and the run detail's 5.00 is 33.9 pt. The gap is rank (Rule 2), not size. The art direction for the mockups (`grill2/art-direction.md`, point 3: poster type at 56–120 px for the hero word or number) is larger than any Home hero in the sample. That size belongs to Rule 5.
- **Proposed wording:** "**Hero register.** A hero figure is Archivo at 40 px (48 px at most), its caption 14 px under it. A hero headline is 25 px, or 31 px when it also names the page. Nothing on Home, Train, Food or Analytics is larger than 48 px."

### Rule 5. The display register (64–120 px) is for four moments, never for Home or a list

- **Rule:** Type above 48 px (ink 4× body or more) appears only on finish summaries, new-best and milestone reveals, streak reveals, and live tracking readouts. It is set at 64–120 px (ink 48–90 pt): one display figure per screen (a live screen may stack up to three readouts), and never a zero or a dash.
- **Evidence:** 10 of 79 reference screens have text at 4× body or more, and none of them is a Home screen (0 of 30).

Use Archivo at 64, 96 or 120 px, one figure per screen, with its caption under it. It is never used on Home, Train, Food, a list or a card, and never for a zero, a dash or a placeholder."

### Rule 6. One display-set element per screen, inside the hero, in Archivo

- **Rule:** At most one element per screen is set in the display face: Archivo at a wide width and heavy weight (two when a figure and its headline are a pair). It sits inside the hero or on the hero's own primary button. Section headings, card titles, rows, other buttons and body text never take it.
- **Evidence:** 9 of 18 fitness apps have a display register beyond their text face's normal weights, and those apps use it on most of their main screens.

- **Proposed wording:** "**One display line a screen.** The display face is Archivo, wide and heavy (`.poster`). A screen uses it once, for the hero's headline or figure (twice when the two are a pair, such as 'First 5K' over '24:51'), and may use it on the hero's Start. Section headings, card titles, rows, other buttons and body text never use it. Sentences stay in Plus Jakarta Sans."

### Rule 7. Six text sizes at most, on a scale with two figure-only steps above it

- **Rule:** One screen uses at most six text sizes, counting sizes within 15% as one. Four of them are caption, body, card title and section heading. The fifth and sixth are the hero step and, on a Rule 5 moment, one display step. The steps above 31 px are figures and display lines only.
- **Evidence:** sizes per reference screen: median 5 (IQR 4–6), range 1–8; 74 of 79 screens use six or fewer. The five that use more are WHOOP's and Apple Fitness's day-1 screens and three Hevy and Fitbod detail screens. How the sizes sit against body (ink ÷ body ink), and the share of screens that use each band:

  | Band                               | Share of reference screens        |
  | ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
  | 0.75–0.9× (caption)                | 65%                               |
  | 1.0× (body)                        | 97%                               |
  | 1.1–1.25× (card title)             | 61%                               |
  | 1.25–1.45× (section)               | 58%                               |
  | 1.45–2.0× (title or hero headline) | 47% at 1.45–1.7×, 33% at 1.7–2.0× |
  | 2.0–3.2× (hero figure)             | 18% at 2.0–2.5×, 15% at 2.5–3.2×  |
  | 3.2× and up (display)              | 19% (15 of 79)                    |

  Tropos's bands match the references up to 3.2× and are empty above it (0 of 18 captures).

- **Why it works:** a few steps far apart read as a hierarchy. Many steps close together read as noise, which is why the Hevy and Fitbod details at 7 sizes read as busy lists.
- **Tropos today:** median 5 sizes (IQR 4–6). Three captures go over: new-account Train (7), the logger (7) and Analytics (8). The 1.25 modular scale in §4 (12, 14, 16, 20, 25, 31, 48) is sound, but 48 is its ceiling and it has no step for display.
- **Proposed wording:** "**Scale.** Text steps: 12, 14, 16, 20, 25, 31 px. Figure-only steps: hero 40 and 48, display 64, 96 and 120 (Archivo). A screen uses at most six sizes: caption, body, card title, section heading, one hero step and, on a finish, reveal or live screen, one display step."

### Rule 8. At hero size the unit is a caption or a smaller word on the figure's baseline

- **Rule:** A figure of 24 pt ink or more either moves its meaning and unit to a caption line under it, or keeps the unit inline in the text face at 0.4–0.7 of the figure's ink height, on the same baseline. A target ("/ 2,930") is smaller and muted. A same-size unit appears only in small stat rows (15 pt ink or less).
- **Evidence:** of 22 reference hero figures at 24 pt ink or more, 18 carry their meaning on a caption line or need no unit (Oura's READINESS, NRC's "Miles" under 2.02, Duolingo's "day streak", MacroFactor's "Remaining"), and 4 keep a unit or target inline. Measured unit ÷ figure height: WHOOP "1:39 hr" 0.47 ([whoop-trend-strength-6m](https://mobbin.com/screens/e2217a0e-7bb4-4aac-9edb-20a890bd9788)), Bevel "67%" 0.67 ([bevel-sleep-detail](https://mobbin.com/screens/ec59eceb-c06f-4ac6-b132-1938e39bac5c)), Cal AI "2471 /3264" 0.39 and grey ([calai-home-dark-week-rings](https://mobbin.com/screens/0b587652-d78b-4abb-8d03-aa9685167524)), Runna's all-capitals "0.96KM" 0.94. Smaller figures follow the same pattern: Apple's 23 pt "46/250CAL" 0.68 in small capitals ([apple-fitness-day-ring-partial-weekstrip](https://mobbin.com/screens/527bed87-fe98-4e4d-b778-b635c9475df5)), MyFitnessPal's "963 cal / 2,930" 0.69 and grey. Every inline unit sits on the figure's baseline (checked on `crops/U1–U2.png`). Same-size units appear only in capitals (Runna) or in stat rows at 12–15 pt ink (Strava "20.47 km", Hevy "5,895 kg").
- **Why it works:** the number is the news and the unit is grammar. A smaller unit on the baseline keeps the figure's shape whole and still reads as one fact.
- **Tropos today:** already compliant. Run detail "5.00 km" measures 0.45, Home "70.0 kg" about 0.55, Food "410" sits over "kcal left", and the weekly review "13,280" over "kg lifted". §4 (Units) and §14c say much the same. This rule adds the numbers and the caption option for display figures.
- **Proposed wording (add to §4 Units):** "At 40 px and above, put the unit in the figure's caption ('kg lifted', 'km', 'day streak'). If it must stay inline, set it in the text face at 40–70% of the figure's height, on its baseline. A target after a slash is smaller and muted ('1,065 / 2,330'). Display figures (64 px and up) always take the caption form."

### Rule 9. On day 1 the hero keeps its size, figure and place

- **Rule:** An empty hero renders at its full size and in its place, with its figure at hero size: "0 of 3", "--", the goal. A centred icon over a 20 px headline does not count as a hero.
- **Evidence:** in five of six pairs of filled and day-1 screens, the hero's type stays within 15% of its filled size. WHOOP's dials are 19.7 → 19.5 pt ("--%", [whoop-home-new-user-get-started](https://mobbin.com/screens/a04b3fb9-cae0-4dd7-8b46-42403add333a)), Oura 37.7 → 37.7 ([oura-today-first-readiness-score](https://mobbin.com/screens/5a37255c-77fd-4e23-884b-5d9af68a0a38)), Cal AI 28.5 → 32.7 ("2900 left", [calai-home-day1-empty](https://mobbin.com/screens/928beeca-5bc5-4dfd-bc7a-d52753bb359a)), Lifesum 29.3 → 29.0 ([lifesum-diary-day1-recommended](https://mobbin.com/screens/1086deda-e299-44d9-87ac-48e336d1f6c4)), and Runna 17.7 → 17.7 ([runna-today-new-plan-awaits](https://mobbin.com/screens/24c5d161-e6b3-44e4-9df7-daa205e0d024)). The exception is Ladder, whose day-1 headline drops from 28.7 to 16.3 pt but sits on a bigger photo zone (64%). The counter-example is Hevy's empty Home: grey skeletons and no hero, largest text 11.3 pt ([hevy-home-empty-feed](https://mobbin.com/screens/ecbaee88-ef5e-4f2c-9f8f-9abdc27afbf9)).
- **Why it works:** the empty hero teaches the layout the person will live in, and says what will appear there. Every new user lives in this state first (CLAUDE.md).
- **Tropos today:** Home keeps it ("Upper A" 20 pt against "Pull A" 19 pt). Empty Analytics drops from a 34.5 pt figure to a hexagon icon over a 16 pt headline (`current/new-history-dark-fold.png`). Social Feed and the profile use the same hexagon block.
- **Proposed wording (add to §10a):** "An empty hero is still the hero: the same size, the same place, its figure at hero size ('0 of 3 this week', '--'), and one sentence that says when it fills. The hexagon-icon block is for lists, not heroes."

## 2. Rules we should NOT copy

1. **A serif or any third face for headlines** (Oura 5 of 6 screens, Lifesum's reward). It is Oura's brand, and §0.4 rules it out. Archivo's width and italic axes give a display voice inside the two faces.
2. **The display face on page titles and section headings** (MacroFactor's "DASHBOARD" and "STRATEGY", Ladder's "TEAMMATES WORKING OUT", Yazio's black headings). It makes the chrome outrank the hero (MacroFactor's 21.3 pt title beats its own hero on 2 of 4 screens, against Rule 2), and capitals on headings read as shouting.
3. **Units in capitals at the figure's size** (Runna "0.96KM" at 0.94, Apple's "CAL" in small capitals). §4 writes units in their own case ("km", "kcal"). Only Runna's live screen does it at hero size.
4. **Heroes made only of image, with the largest text in a card title** (Strava's feed and You pages, where the map or photo is the hero and "Morning Run" at 14 pt is the largest text, [strava-home-feed](https://mobbin.com/screens/f9216faa-47b8-4b6c-9e69-6464b21c3d74); Apple Fitness+'s photo carousel). A new user has no route and no photo, so the hero is empty on day 1 and the hierarchy falls back to the cards. A route map hero is fine on a run that has one.
5. **A small hero that relies on traffic-light colour** (WHOOP's 18% dial row). It needs green, yellow and red state colours, which the closed palette does not have. Copying the size without the colour gives Tropos's current problem.
6. **A large title over equal cards** (Garmin's greeting, MyFitnessPal's "Today": the two apps in the set people tolerate). That is Tropos Home today.
7. **Giant type on Home** (the art direction's 56–120 px hero word). No loved app does it on its daily screen: the Home maximum is 37.7 pt ink, about 50 pt type. Giant type belongs to the moments in Rule 5, where it reads as an event because it is rare.

## 3. Open questions for the owner

1. **What names Home?**
   - (A) Drop the "Today" title. The date becomes a 12–14 px kicker and the session name is the largest text. This is the Runna, Oura and Ladder pattern: 40 of 80 screens have no separate title or let the hero carry it.
   - (B) Keep "Today" as a small 17 px title row. This is the Apple Fitness and Strava pattern (18 of 80): familiar to iOS users, but it spends about 40 pt above the hero.
2. **Is the display register in capitals?**
   - (A) Uppercase Archivo, wide and heavy, as NRC, Ladder and MacroFactor do (4 apps, and Runna live). It needs a third place for capitals in §4, beyond table headers and the wordmark.
   - (B) Sentence-case display, as Oura and Lifesum do (with numerals as in Duolingo and Gentler). It fits §4 as written, and gets loud through size and width alone.
3. **How heavy is a hero figure?**
   - (A) Keep Archivo 800 at every size. Tropos's hero figures measure a stroke ÷ height of 0.16–0.22, like NRC, Yazio and Duolingo.
   - (B) Let figures above 40 px drop to 500–600. The references' hero figures have a median stroke ratio of 0.174 (about semibold), and Oura, Lifesum, Cal AI and Apple set them at regular (0.12–0.14). This reads as an instrument and gives the display register (Rule 5) more contrast to step up to.
