## The three rules that make the shape

1. **Sets are cut before lifts, down to 2 each** (sessionFit.ts:1-48, `MIN_FITTED_SETS = 2` at :75, `fitDay` at :229). Order: isolations to 2 sets, other compounds to 2, drop some isolations, main lifts to 2. The header states the intent: "Sets are cut before exercises so a short session keeps its calf, side-delt and ab work while it can" (Lift4 (5), Lift11). Result: a short session is wide and shallow by design.

2. **A plan the person already has never loses a lift** (`refitSessionsToTime`, sessionFit.ts:402: "nothing is dropped"). Every lift goes to its role's sets and is cut to 2 until the day fits or nothing is left to cut. Reproduced (`zzCoreKept`):

   | Kept day | After a re-fit to 60 min                            |
   | -------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
   | 8 lifts  | 8 lifts, every one at 2 sets, 65 min, does not fit  |
   | 10 lifts | 10 lifts, every one at 2 sets, 79 min, does not fit |
   | 11 lifts | 11 lifts, every one at 2 sets, 87 min, does not fit |

   This is the owner's "15 different exercises each with 2 sets": a day that holds many lifts (added by hand, or kept from an older, longer plan) is flattened to 2 sets across the board, keeps every lift, and still runs over. There is no cap on lifts in a day and nothing tells the person the day is over. His own Pull A, from his screenshots of 11 November 2026, is 12 lifts at 2 sets each, priced at 93 to 99 minutes (`evidence/owner-pull-a.txt`; README section 2).

3. **A set is priced at about four minutes** (expressSession.ts:125-130, restTime.ts:15-32). Per lift 90 s of setup plus warm-ups; per set 45 s of work plus rest of 180 s (main lift at 6 reps or fewer), 150 s (main), 120 s (compound), 75 s (isolation). Two main lifts at 3x4 cost about 33 of the 60 minutes. The rests are the literature's, and they are why 60 minutes holds 12 to 15 sets where the programmes and apps read in `market-lifting.md` hold 15 to 20.

Two more that compound it:

4. **Heavy and light days are applied to every goal** (`applyDayRoles`, programEngine.ts:1473; "INVISIBLE", :1470). In a Build-muscle week the A days start every main lift at sets of 4 with 3-minute rests. That reads, and costs time, as a strength day.

5. **The split cannot be chosen** (Pgm5 Q1; planBuilder.ts:163-168, programTypes.ts:20-36). `preferredSplit` is stored and ignored: 3 days is always full body, 4 upper/lower, 5 push/pull/legs + upper/lower, 6 push/pull/legs twice. Verified across all five preference values.

## What is not wrong

- No built day runs over its time, and no day exceeds 18 sets.
- Main lifts are never dropped and are spread sensibly (a squat and a hinge pattern each lower day, a press and a pull each upper day).
- A strong lifter's longer warm-ups cost little: doubling every load moved one accessory from 3 sets to 2 (`zzCoreKept`).
