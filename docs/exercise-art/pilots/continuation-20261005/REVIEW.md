# Bulgarian split-squat continuation review

**Decision: keep the selected six-frame set as an inactive draft.** This review
does not grant a production release. It separates native artwork findings from
repository integrity and actual app-player evidence.

## Selected work

The frozen setup was recreated from the owner's interrupted-run screenshot and
the canonical anatomy reference. The supporting rear hip/thigh is neutral; the
working front quadriceps remains purple. The later poses show an initial knee
bend, a distinct deeper intermediate and a near-horizontal front thigh at the
bottom. The repaired intermediate shows the far fingers and handle clearly.

The final draft uses setup → shallow → deep → bottom → deep → shallow. The last
part of the ascent is frame 6 → frame 1. Source selection, six separate paths,
dimensions, native hashes, reuse and seven-word cues are bound by `MANIFEST.json`
and `bulgarian-split/provenance.json`.

The bottom support compositor restores the original bench and both shoes. Its
initial ankle cuts and later tongue-line artifacts were repaired with documented
local masks. The selected bottom adds only 2,374 reviewed lilac pixels from the
generated hamstring colour repair; equipment, hands and support pixels are
unchanged by that import. The deeper intermediate has a separate rear-shoe
repair, using its own calf geometry rather than the bottom bridge.

The frozen deeper repair still has a short angular shelf on its upper calf/ankle
contour at approximately x848–861, y599–604 (about ±3px annotation uncertainty).
It is clearest in the 3× crop and can be seen at native size when inspecting that
area. The calf remains connected, without an open gap or doubled outline, and
the original shoe/contact is preserved. This residual contour finding remains
open; exact contact coordinates do not resolve it.

## Rejected equipment repair

The rigid-equipment experiment copied one setup-derived weight layer through the
poses. Twelve sampled face-interior comparisons were exact, but native review
found harder edges in shallow/deep and a stepped dark strip, old-edge remnant and
flat cutout around the bottom hand/thigh. Those images were rejected for the
selected sequence. The original clean equipment contours are retained in the
main draft, with their dimensional uncertainty explicitly unresolved.

Two attempts to generate only the missing exterior anatomy instead changed the
larger scene or body pose. Neither output was imported, rescaled into place, or
used to claim a fixed geometry pass. Original attempts, exact prompts, masks,
measurements and rejection findings remain beside the candidate.

## Continuation point

Resolve the equipment silhouette and its anatomical occlusion together, retaining
both readable grips and fixed supports. Rerun native diagnostics on the actual
selected hashes after any image change. Then run and inspect the unchanged
three-check mobile suite in a browser-equipped environment. The browser blocker
and complete future release checklist are preserved under `validation/`.

The complete guide remains outside `public/` and the release registry. Released
coverage remains 64 of 141; this draft is not counted as a newly released guide.
