## Historical findings superseded by V2

The first rigid-equipment experiment copied one setup-derived weight layer through the
poses. Twelve sampled face-interior comparisons were exact, but native review
found harder edges in shallow/deep and a stepped dark strip, old-edge remnant and
flat cutout around the bottom hand/thigh. Those images were rejected for the
earlier selected sequence. Its originally generated near-head chords measured
approximately 67 / 69 / 67 / 73px with approximately ±2px span uncertainty. That
evidence could not support a sub-percent invariance pass. The V1 selected hashes
and findings remain in `validation/final-selection.json`; their equipment is now
superseded by the separately reviewed V2 construction rather than retrospectively
declared passing.

Two attempts to generate only the missing exterior anatomy instead changed the
larger scene or body pose. Neither output was imported, rescaled into place, or
used to claim a fixed geometry pass. Original attempts, exact prompts, masks,
measurements and rejection findings remain beside the candidate. V2 instead uses
one complete transparent asset without attached anatomy. Its slightly larger
fixed projections cover the old equipment, while original foreground contours
preserve fingers and anatomical occlusion. A separate early V2 fingertip-mask
trial and its correction are also retained in the V2 evidence directory.
