# Front squat front-rack feasibility review

Status: rejected pilot; do not route or release.

Source: `docs/exercise-art/pilots/redraw-20261027/front-squat/1.png`. The
existing six-frame draft has coherent squat depth and almost fixed shoe and
plate geometry, but the bar crosses the throat and both elbows stay low and
tucked. That is an incorrect front-rack position at every depth.

`rejected/standing-rack-edit.png` is a direct image edit generated from the
standing source. The edit was explicitly instructed to put the bar on the
front deltoids below the throat and raise both elbows forward to shoulder
height. It retained low elbows and a bar at the neck, and changed plate shape
and hand positions. It therefore cannot establish a correct starting pose,
much less a consistent six-frame sequence.

The existing accurate procedural `BODY_DEMOS["front-squat"]` remains the
better form guide. Further illustrated work needs pose control for the front
rack throughout the squat and locked equipment geometry. The current draft
must not be approved based on the small pixel drift alone.
