# New conversions — batch 04

Two previously unconverted catalogue exercises: Seated Calf Raise and Farmer’s Carry. Twelve frame slots, seven unique native generated poses. These are review drafts, not production releases.

## Seated Calf Raise

The selected machine has its plate and thigh pads forward of a rear pivot, correcting the first candidate’s opposing load arrangement. Three poses show neutral, raised, and top positions, then reverse. The initial rejected machine is retained separately.

Release blockers: the bottom does not yet show a full below-step heel stretch; mid and top ankle angles are too similar; thigh-pad travel does not track the plate’s lever rotation consistently. Fixed supports also need a continuity review.

## Farmer’s Carry

Four poses show pickup, upright hold, and two alternating steps. Standing and walking keep the torso upright and the weights at the sides. Frames five and six reuse standing and pickup for the controlled stop/set-down.

Release blockers: the pickup’s body and dumbbells change scale compared with standing; the dumbbells do not clearly touch the floor in the pickup. Walking step width and ground contact need correction before publication.
