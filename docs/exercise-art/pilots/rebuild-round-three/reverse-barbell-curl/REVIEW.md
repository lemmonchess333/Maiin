# Reverse barbell curl: frontal rebuild

Six candidate frame files, three unique native poses. The new symmetric front master changes the failed earlier three-quarter generation method. The start and high position show continuous wrist/forearm lines and an overhand grip. Equal modest plates, shaft, head, torso and shoes remain broadly consistent without a visible background halo.

The midpoint looks compatible with fingers wrapping down around the shaft, but the front camera hides the sagittal forearm axis. Neutral wrists cannot be independently established at that point; do not count the original wrist issue as conclusively fixed. Top shoulder/upper-arm contours also change somewhat, making the exact elbows-pinned path difficult to establish. These are draft candidates, not approved replacements.

Independent review by review_rebuilds inspected master before midpoint, midpoint before top, then the top. No definite midpoint wrist flexion was asserted solely from visible fingers. Review accepts retaining the sequence for further inspection, not release approval.

Frames 1 and 6 are the identical low/reset position; 2 and 5 the identical midpoint on ascent/descent; 3 and 4 the identical high position with an explicit brief hold. This reuse is intentional physical pose reuse, not six unique image-generation claims. Frames preserve full 1024 by 1536 native resolution.
