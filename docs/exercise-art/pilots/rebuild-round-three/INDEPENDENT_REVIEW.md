## Reverse Barbell Curl

Exact catalogue variation is a standing straight-bar curl with an overhand shoulder-width grip, wrists straight and elbows by the sides. The new starting master visibly establishes this grip and modest equal plates. It was accepted only as a candidate to generate the next pose.

The midpoint keeps head, torso, shoes and equipment broadly consistent. Fingers wrapping down around a horizontal shaft are not alone evidence of wrist flexion. Conversely, a front camera hides the sagittal forearm axis, so this image cannot prove that the earlier wrist problem was repaired. Apparent forearm shortening may be foreshortening and is not independently labelled anatomical distortion.

The top shows the bar near shoulder level and dorsal hands continuous with the forearms. Shoulder/upper-arm contours change; exact elbows-pinned travel is still difficult to establish from this angle. No obvious background halo or broken grip is visible in the three poses. Plate size and shaft remain broadly consistent; this is visual comparison, not a calibrated equipment measurement.

Frames 4, 5 and 6 intentionally repeat 3, 2 and 1. Their hold, lowering and reset meanings agree with that reuse. This is a six-file draft with three joint configurations, not six unique generated poses. The return endpoint equals the start, but smooth timing and the app loop have not been tested. Technique cues are instructions, not proof that a hidden wrist angle is correct.

## Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift

The corrected starting master presents two dumbbells at the thighs and a useful side view of the hinge plane. The far grip is partially occluded. Hamstrings have the strongest purple, with lighter secondary posterior-chain highlights; the calf tint from the first master is removed. Full shoes and headroom are visible. The floor line crosses behind the shoes and is not itself a literal sole contact baseline.

Both subsequent images show a recognizable hip hinge with weights close to the legs. However, both shoe footprints shift right/up relative to the starting master, and the horizontal floor reference also shifts upward. This is a fixed-scene change, not merely a changed projection of a moving limb. The correction retains substantially the same failed anchors. Stopping before a bottom pose avoids multiplying a failed master-to-motion method. The six cue beats remain a production plan; only the starting image exists as a numbered frame.

## Reverse Flyes

The catalogue requires a hip hinge with torso near parallel to the floor, neutral dumbbells hanging below the chest and soft elbows. The first proposed rear-view master depicts an upright standing athlete. The next depicts a rotated/asymmetric torso and does not establish the required hinge. Neither is a valid exercise starting master; generating raised-arm poses from them would not resolve the variation mismatch. Both rejections are independently supported by the still images. The prior six-frame draft and its asymmetry issue remain unresolved; these are rejected attempts, not a replacement sequence.
