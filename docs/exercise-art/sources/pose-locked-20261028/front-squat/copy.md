# Front Squat barbell placard copy

Six frames use depth progress `[0, 0.28, 0.65, 1, 0.65, 0.28]`.
All cues are seven words or fewer.

| Frame |    t | Label      | Cue                                          |
| ----- | ---: | ---------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| 1     |    0 | Front rack | Bar on front delts; elbows high.             |
| 2     | 0.28 | Descend    | Brace; bend knees with heels planted.        |
| 3     | 0.65 | Lower      | Lower upright; keep elbows pointing forward. |
| 4     |    1 | Depth      | Reach depth; keep chest and elbows lifted.   |
| 5     | 0.65 | Rise       | Drive upward through your whole feet.        |
| 6     | 0.28 | Return     | Keep elbows high on final ascent.            |

## Authored setup instructions

1. Set the bar across the front delts, then cross your forearms and rest each hand on the bar toward the opposite shoulder. Keep your elbows high; your shoulders support the bar.
2. Stand shoulder-width with toes slightly out, whole feet grounded, and core braced.
3. Squat between your heels until thighs reach or slightly pass parallel. Rise with an upright torso and the bar over midfoot.

## Tip

Keep crossed arms high so the bar stays on the front delts and clear of your throat. Stop the set if the rack collapses.

## Common mistakes

- Letting elbows drop so the bar rolls toward the throat.
- Lifting heels or letting knees collapse inward.
- Pitching the torso forward and losing the brace.
