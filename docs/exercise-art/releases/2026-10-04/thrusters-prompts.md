## Reproducing fixed-contact repair and framing

Copy the five PNGs from docs/exercise-art/masters/thrusters/sources into a scratch directory. Run thrusters-sole-repair.cjs followed by thrusters-framing.cjs, each with that directory as its first argument, using the repository sharp dependency. Only two stationary sole rectangles are repaired; all moving anatomy is unchanged. The provenance records the exact rectangles and zero changes outside them. The final six PNGs are encoded losslessly to the delivered WebP assets.
