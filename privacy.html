TROPOS

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 March 2026

Tropos is a fitness tracking app for hybrid athletes. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the control you have over your information.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address and password (or authentication tokens if you sign in with Google or Apple)
  • Your name

Profile and body data

To personalise your training programme and calorie targets, we collect:

  • Date of birth or age range
  • Gender
  • Height and weight
  • Fitness goals, experience level, training preferences, and injury information (from onboarding)

Activity and health data

When you use the app, we collect:

  • Workout logs (exercises, sets, reps, weights, duration)
  • Run data (distance, pace, duration, route via GPS)
  • Food and nutrition logs (calories, macronutrients), including via barcode scanning
  • Water intake
  • Daily health score
  • Apple HealthKit or Google Health Connect data (steps, sleep, heart rate) — only when you explicitly grant permission

Photos

If you use the food scanning feature, photos of your meals are sent to Google Vertex AI (Gemini) for nutritional analysis. These photos are processed in real time and are not stored by the AI service after analysis is complete. See Section 3 for more detail.

Progress photos are stored securely in Firebase Storage with AES-256-GCM encryption and are only visible to you unless you explicitly choose to share them.

Location data

When you use the run tracking feature, we collect GPS data to map your route. Location data is only collected while you are actively recording a run and the app is in use. We do not track your location in the background. You can define privacy zones (such as your home address) to automatically blur GPS data near sensitive locations when sharing activities with other users.

Local data caching

Tropos uses Firestore persistent cache (IndexedDB) to store data locally on your device for offline access. This cached data allows the app to function without an internet connection and is synced when connectivity is restored.

2. How we use your data

We use your information to:

  • Provide and personalise the Tropos service — including generating your training programme, calculating calorie and macro targets, and tracking your progress
  • Analyse food photos and meal descriptions using AI to estimate nutritional content
  • Calculate your TDEE and energy balance based on your profile, activity level, and training phase
  • Display your run routes on a map
  • Sync health data from Apple HealthKit or Google Health Connect
  • Power social features including your activity feed, follow relationships, and group interactions
  • Send you notifications — including streak reminders, workout completion alerts, protein target check-ins, new follower alerts, kudos and comment notifications, and weekly summaries (if you opt in)
  • Improve the app through aggregated, anonymised usage analytics

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not serve ads. We do not share your data with advertisers.

3. AI-powered features

Tropos uses Google Vertex AI (Gemini 2.0 Flash) to power nutritional analysis features:

  • Food photo scanning: When you take a photo of a meal, the image is sent as base64-encoded data to Google Vertex AI via Cloud Functions. The AI analyses the image and returns estimated calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat. Photos are processed in real time and are not retained by the AI service after analysis.
  • Text-based meal analysis: If you describe a meal in text, the description is sent to Google Vertex AI for nutritional estimation.

Google Vertex AI processes this data under Google Cloud's data processing terms. Tropos does not use your food photos or descriptions to train AI models.

4. Social features

Tropos includes social features that allow you to connect with other users:

  • Activity sharing: You can share workout and run activities with other users. Visibility is configurable — you can set activities to public, followers-only, or private.
  • Follow relationships: Other users can follow you (and you can follow them) to see shared activities in their feed. A public discovery feed shows activities from users who have set their content to public.
  • Kudos and comments: Users can give kudos (similar to "likes") and leave comments on shared activities.
  • Crews and groups: You can join or create groups with leaderboards and shared challenges.
  • User search: Other users can find your profile by searching your name or email address.
  • Privacy zones: You can define geographic privacy zones (e.g. your home) to automatically obscure GPS data near those locations when sharing run activities.
  • Content moderation: You can report or block other users. We review reported content and take action in accordance with our community guidelines.

You can control the visibility of your activities and profile at any time in Settings.

5. Data storage and security

Your data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), which is hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure. Firebase provides encryption at rest and in transit.

Progress photos are stored in Firebase Storage with AES-256-GCM encryption, providing an additional layer of protection for sensitive personal images.

Account authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication, which supports industry-standard security practices including encrypted password storage and OAuth 2.0 for third-party sign-in providers.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain it.

6. Third-party services

Tropos uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, file storage, hosting, and Cloud Functions
  • Google Vertex AI (Gemini) — food photo and text analysis for nutritional estimation
  • Apple HealthKit / Google Health Connect — health data sync (only with your explicit permission)
  • RevenueCat — subscription and in-app purchase management
  • MapLibre GL — client-side map rendering for run routes (no data sent to external servers)

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them. We only share the minimum data necessary for each service to function.

7. HealthKit and Health Connect

If you grant Tropos permission to access Apple HealthKit or Google Health Connect data:

  • We read steps, sleep, and heart rate data to enhance your health score and activity tracking
  • We do not write data back to HealthKit or Health Connect without your permission
  • HealthKit and Health Connect data is not shared with third parties or used for advertising
  • This data is stored securely alongside your other Tropos data in Firebase

8. Your rights and choices

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data — you can view all your data within the app
  • Correct your data — you can update your profile, body metrics, and preferences at any time in Settings
  • Delete your account and all associated data — contact us or use the account deletion option in Settings
  • Revoke permissions — you can revoke HealthKit, Health Connect, location, camera, and notification permissions at any time through your device settings
  • Export your data — you can export workouts, meals, and bodyweight data as CSV, and runs as GPX, directly from Settings. For a complete data export, contact us.

If you are in the UK or EU, you have additional rights under GDPR, including the right to object to processing and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

9. Children's privacy

Tropos is not intended for children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 16, we will take steps to delete that data promptly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you through the app or by email. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.
