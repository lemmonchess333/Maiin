Support

Tropos is an adaptive fitness app for lifting, running, and nutrition tracking. If you need help, have a question, or want to report a problem, we're here.

Contact us

Email us and we'll get back to you, usually within 2–3 business days:

support@troposfit.com

To help us resolve your issue quickly, please include the device and operating system you're using (for example, iPhone 15, iOS 18) and a short description of what happened.

Common questions

  • Managing your subscription. Pro subscriptions are managed through your device's App Store settings. On iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel there at any time.
  • Deleting your account. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from within the app: Settings → Delete Account. All personal data is removed within 30 days.
  • Exporting your data. You can export your data in a standard format from the app's settings.
  • Privacy and your data. See our Privacy Policy for how your data is collected, used, and protected.

Health disclaimer

Tropos is a fitness tracking tool, not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making changes to your diet or exercise routine.
